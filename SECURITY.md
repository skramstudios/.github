# Security policy

Skram, Skram Vault, and Skram Tunnel are distributed as closed-source
binaries. The source is not published, so we cannot accept a patch — but we
do want to know about a vulnerability, and a public issue is the wrong place
to report one.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Use GitHub's private vulnerability reporting on the affected product's own
public repository: open the repository, go to the **Security** tab, and
select **Report a vulnerability**.

- [skram](https://github.com/skramstudios/skram/security)
- [skram-vault](https://github.com/skramstudios/skram-vault/security)
- [skram-tunnel](https://github.com/skramstudios/skram-tunnel/security)

Please include:

- which product and which version (`<command> version`)
- your OS and architecture
- what you did, what you expected, and what happened
- whatever you can share about impact and how to reproduce it

We do not make promises about response times or offer a bounty. Please do
not open a public issue for a suspected vulnerability.
